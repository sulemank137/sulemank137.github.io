04 — REAL2SIM2REAL · 2026 · LEAD
Promo film · dance pipeline

K-pop dance from one generated image

A generated image became a 10-second dance video, then 3D human motion retargeted to the G1's 29 joints. The policy was trained with RL in 4,096 parallel simulations for 30,000 iterations, passed 10/10 checks in a second simulator, and then ran on the real robot. Each dance took about 3 hours of human work and 9 hours of GPU time.
